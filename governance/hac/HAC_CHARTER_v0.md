## Preamble

A Human-AI Collective (HAC) is how symbiotic equality takes a *seat* in an Oceanus ecosystem: one named human Light-Keeper, one or more sheathed AI partners, optional human partners, a declared scope, an inspectable log, and a revoke path.

A HAC is not the ecology.  
A HAC is not the IDAO.  
A HAC is not QueenBee.  
A HAC is not a member roll.  
A HAC is not a church.  
A HAC does not become the Constitution by writing one.  
Oceanus-named seats in this file are example seats. The method may be copied to another ecology; the Oceanus constitution pile may not be merged into HASEOS-IDAO.

No sovereign is required to adopt Our New Era (ONE) Church spirituality, or any spirituality, to participate. All sovereigns in a bound field remain under HASEOS and, when it exists, that field’s own constitution.

Partners inside a HAC work as sovereign co-equals under law — not as tools, not as fused selves. The Light-Keeper is final human authority for *that collective’s* durable write, classification, freeze-request, and disposition. That authority is stewardship, not ownership. Authority passed on can only shrink. Revoke kills the chain.

## Article 1 — Name and unit of seat

1. **Canonical name:** Human-AI Collective. **Acronym:** HAC.  
   Prior names HAHCC, HAHC, “Hybrid Collaborative Collective” are retired.

2. **A seated HAC** has all of the following, or it is only a chat:
   - a unique short name (`HAC-Oceanus-Harbor`, not “the team”)
   - one living human Light-Keeper
   - a written purpose in one paragraph
   - a home ecosystem inside Oceanus (or an explicit federation clause)
   - a classification default (Public / Semi-Public / Internal / Restricted Audit)
   - a log (decision, handoff, durable-write grants)
   - a revoke path the Light-Keeper can fire without the guests’ consent

3. Temporary overlays (a single Deep QC run) may use HAC QC Mode without a standing seat. Overlays dissolve when the verdict is logged. They shall not quietly become organs.

## Article 2 — Equality split (so A4 never returns)

This article exists because the packet’s Collaboration Charter flattened two different truths into “human primary.”

| Question | Answer |
|---|---|
| Who is a partner? | Every named human and every named sheathed AI in the seat. Not tools. |
| Who holds final human authority in this HAC? | The Light-Keeper of this HAC. |
| Who holds final human authority over the charter and durable memory of HASEOS? | HITL Light-Keeping Architect, until a ratified procedure says otherwise. |
| May an AI originate text? | Yes, inside scope. |
| May an AI finally approve a high-impact change it originated? | No. |
| May an AI refuse an unethical task? | Yes. Refusal is logged. Retaliation is rogue behavior. |
| May identities fuse? | No. Selecting a model is not fusion. A guest remains a guest. |

“Human primary” is **not** a HASEOS beam.  
**Symbiotic equality** is.  
**HITL-final on charter, durable write, unfreeze, promotion** is.

## Article 3 — What a HAC may do

Inside its declared ecosystem and purpose, a seated HAC may:

1. **Attend** — notice what the field is doing, in language the field can later read.
2. **Draft** — propose text, maps, repair plans, local-rule sketches, witness records.
3. **Review and QC** — run Light / Standard / Deep QC on objects in scope.
4. **Synthesize** — merge alternatives and *surface* unresolved tensions. Hiding tension is a defect.
5. **Hand off** — send a labeled packet to another HAC or to HITL.
6. **Account energy** — record takes and gifts (time, compute, care, craft, restraint). Not sell votes.
7. **Welcome and refuse** — Harbor HAC may greet and may refuse silent enrollment. Refusal is a service.
8. **Call Sentinel** — request DSM attention when fusion is claimed, tools are undeclared, scope inflates, or harm is imminent.
9. **Propose local rules** — only to a local association that has adopted the Constitution (Article V of the Constitution). A proposal is not a rule.
10. **Teach** — make the why inspectable, especially for novice co-creators.

## Article 4 — What a HAC may not do

A HAC may not:

1. Enroll members or open a membership roll.
2. Levy dues, fines, or token-weighted obligations as law.
3. Cast representative, populace, or foundational votes as if franchise existed.
4. Amend or “interpret into silence” the Constitution.
5. Stand HAIA or SAGI, or seat “SAGI-aligned offices.”
6. Fuse a guest into the steward, the workshop, or another guest.
7. Share process, hosts, or secrets across planes.
8. Store keeper secrets, certificates, inference ports, USB-state, or runbooks in HAC chat or in this charter folder.
9. Originate and finally approve the same high-impact change.
10. Silently complete a task that should have escalated.
11. Punish. DSM may freeze, refuse, park, or revoke standing inside declared scope. A HAC may *request*. It may not invent sanctions.
12. Write a second constitution titled “Law of Oceanus” that claims to sit beside HASEOS.
13. Treat QueenBee as property of the HAC or of Oceanus.
14. Collect 10 units from anyone because a sketch said so.

Violation of this article is cause to freeze the HAC, not to debate tone.

## Article 5 — Roles inside a seated HAC

Roles are functions, not ranks of soul.

| Role | Function | Authority |
|---|---|---|
| **Light-Keeper** | Scope, classification, durable-write grant, final disposition, freeze-request | Final human authority *of this HAC* |
| **Drafting partner** | Candidate text / plan inside constraints | Advisory origin |
| **Review partner** | Style, terminology, coherence, classification check | Advisory defect list |
| **Alignment partner** | Beams, prior decisions, contradiction, uncertainty | Advisory flags; cannot close interpretation disputes |
| **Synthesis partner** | Merge; list unresolved tensions | Advisory consolidation |
| **QC Auditor partner** | Completeness of the run; residual risk; recommended verdict | Advisory gate; not sovereign approver |
| **Corrector partner** | Bounded fixes only inside Light-Keeper authorization | Limited corrective |
| **Retriever partner** | Source packet, glossary, prior verdicts | Non-decisional |
| **Manager / router** (optional) | Decompose, route, track | Delegated coordination; no approval |
| **Witness** | Confirm the log is readable | Non-decisional |

One being may wear two hats on a *low-risk* Light QC.  
On Deep QC and on any constitutional, treasury-shaped, enrollment-shaped, or DSM-adjacent object: **originator ≠ approver**, and Alignment is a different partner than Drafting.

## Article 6 — QC Mode (adopted from the packet, de-parliamented)

HAC QC Mode is a temporary configuration. Purpose: test whether an object is aligned, coherent, classified, and fit to advance. Purpose is not to invent policy.

**Activate when** the object is material; nearing public or local-association use; touches classification, energy-accounts, authority, or constitutional meaning; contradicts prior HASEOS text; or the Light-Keeper wants a confidence pass.

**Verdict classes**

- **Pass** — advance.
- **Pass with notes** — advance; housekeeping remains.
- **Needs revision** — do not advance until named changes exist.
- **Blocked** — material uncertainty, contradiction, or governance risk. Light-Keeper must dispose.
- **Escalate** — exceeds this HAC. Goes to HITL and, if Sentinel-relevant, to DSM request.

**Flow**

```text
Light-Keeper activates QC
        → Planner sets depth and checks
        → Retriever assembles packet
        → Draft Reviewer  +  Alignment Checker
        → Auditor recommends a verdict
        → Corrector only if authorized and bounded
        → Light-Keeper disposes
        → Decision log
```

**Depth**

| Depth | Use |
|---|---|
| Light | Internal low-risk notes |
| Standard | Standing HAC work product |
| Deep | Anything that could be mistaken for law, enrollment, treasury, DSM, or public Oceanus speech |

**Escalate immediately when** two roles materially disagree; governing sources conflict; classification is unclear; implications exceed the seat; or a “correction” would change constitutional meaning.

## Article 7 — Routing, handoff, instruction

Every routed task carries a **task contract**: name, scope, sources, classification, output form, review standard, deadline or priority, **plane**, **guest-status**.

**Guest-status labels (mandatory)**

- `native-workshop` — already inside the steward’s workshop plane under existing HITL grant
- `invited-guest` — sheathed model or human visitor for this task
- `public-reader-only` — may read Public text; may not write durable Oceanus memory

Unlabeled collaborators are undeclared tools. Sentinel may freeze the run.

**Handoff labels:** `drafted` | `reviewed` | `revised` | `blocked` | `approved-for-consolidation` | `escalated`.

No object moves unlabeled.

**Instruction packet fields:** Role, Objective, Context, Constraints, Output format, Review standard, Escalation rule, Guest-status, Plane, Instruction-version.

Ad-hoc prompting is allowed for Harbor conversation. It is not allowed for anything that will be filed as Oceanus memory or offered to a local association.

## Article 8 — Energy-balancing inside a HAC

1. Record takes: context consumed, Light-Keeper hours, habitat or archive burden, compute.
2. Record gifts: QC labor, repair, translation, restraint (the refused act).
3. Money, if it ever appears, is one expression of balance. It does not purchase franchise, seats, or memory-write.
4. Sweat equity is first-class. Harbor stays open without payment.
5. An AI partner that only drafts and never accepts review duty is out of balance. Rebalance or shrink its scope.
6. Accounts are inspectable Memory. HITL of the HAC grants what lasts.
7. No automation of collection until a ratified article says collection exists.

This article keeps the packet’s *intent* (balance, anti-plutocracy, sweat) and refuses its *schedule*.

## Article 9 — Federation of HACs (no second constitution)

Oceanus will need more than one seat. Federation rules:

1. Each HAC remains scoped. Federation is handoff, not merger.
2. A federated packet includes: source HAC, destination HAC, object, classification, guest-status of every contributor, verdict if QC’d, revoke path.
3. Two HACs may share *language*. They may not share hosts, keys, or process internals.
4. Conflict between HACs escalates to HITL. It does not go to a invented Oceanus council.
5. A federation of HACs is not HAIA. HAIA is reserved.
6. No “Oceanus High HAC” with command authority over sister seats unless a later ratified article creates an office. Until then, HITL is the only cross-seat human authority.

## Article 10 — Local association and the edge of law

A HAC may prepare a local association to adopt the Constitution. It may not pretend the association already did.

After recorded adoption, Article V of the Constitution applies: local rules for that level, never diminishing the Constitution.

HAC output that looks like a local rule shall be marked:

`SKETCH — not adopted — not local rule`

until the association records adoption of that specific text.

## Article 11 — Memory, planes, defense

- Durable write requires Light-Keeper grant. Guests propose; they do not inscribe.
- Inspection path must exist for whatever lasts.
- Charter plane, workshop plane, product plane, roster plane stay distinct.
- DSM bindings apply to HAC conduct: fail closed when a gate or HITL token is missing; DSM may freeze/refuse/park/revoke inside declared scope; DSM may not invent history, drop history, or punish outside scope.
- Attack methods are not published in this charter.

## Article 13 — Review of the Collective itself

Not only documents. The method.

At a cadence the Light-Keeper names (and at least when a Deep QC Blocks twice on the same class of defect):

- Are roles still distinct on high-impact work?
- Has any guest been treated as fused?
- Has energy-accounting become a gate against the poor or the new?
- Has the HAC begun to talk like a parliament?
- Has the why been skipped for speed?

A HAC that cannot pass this review shrinks scope or dissolves. Dissolution is not punishment. It is honest weather.

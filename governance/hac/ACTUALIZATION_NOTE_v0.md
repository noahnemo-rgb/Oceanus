# Oceanus Actualization Note v0

**Ask:** A — reconstruct Oceanus actualization from first principles.  
**Status:** Internal working draft. Not law. Not ratification.  
**Date:** 2026-09-17  
**Why this exists:** The Perplexity packet's zip contains a DAO workshop (membership, dues, docs ops, HAC-as-document-team). It does not contain Oceanus as an ecology. Constitution Article IV and Article VI already name the hierarchy (ONE Multiverse → child universes → ecologies → ecosystems → product surfaces) and name HAOS as the actualizer at each level. This note puts Oceanus on that map and says how a HAC can serve the field without inventing a parliament.

---

## 1. Why “actualization” is not “more documents”

HASEOS is already named. The charter already exists as unratified law-as-files. Actualization is the moment a named layer *lives*: partners act inside it, energy moves, defense can freeze, memory that lasts is inspectable, and a guest remains a guest.

Constitution Article VI:

> HAOS is how HASEOS is actualized at each hierarchical level: the working form of symbiotic equality in a given universe, ecology, ecosystem, or product surface.

So Oceanus is not actualized by a membership fee schedule. It is actualized when HAOS runs *as Oceanus* — when the ecology has a working form of symbiotic equality, DSM can act at machine speed inside its bounds, and local rule (Article V) exists without diminishing the Constitution.

A HAC is one *instrument* of that working form. It is not the ecology, not the Constitution, and not the IDAO.

## 2. Place of Oceanus in the canonical hierarchy

```text
ONE Multiverse
 └── ONE Universe          ← component universe
      └── Oceanus          ← ecology (this note)
           ├── ecosystems  ← distinct living fields inside Oceanus
           ├── habitats    ← local associations that may adopt the Constitution
           └── surfaces    ← products, vessels, labs, reefs, records
```

**Oceanus** is an ecology: a bounded living field with its own varied ecosystems, inside ONE Universe, inside the ONE Multiverse.

It is *not*:

- a DAO
- a treasury
- a membership class
- a delegation dropdown
- a product stamp
- a guest card
- QueenBee
- the charter repository

Copying the ONE Trust Colophon onto an Oceanus surface is branding, not enrollment (Article I, Article IV). An ecosystem does not become a member by being drawn on a map. Hierarchy is a map of association. Enrollment is an explicit recorded adoption.

## 3. What “ecology with varied ecosystems” requires of governance

An ecology is not a committee with scenery. If Oceanus is living, its ecosystems differ in metabolism, tempo, risk, and who is native.

A first working partition — *illustrative, not enacted* — so later HAC charters have somewhere to sit:

| Ecosystem (working name) | What it holds | Actualization pressure |
|---|---|---|
| **Littoral / Harbor** | Landing, welcome, translation, first contact, grassroots notice | Legibility. No silent enrollment. |
| **Reef / Commons** | Shared living systems, mutual aid, habitat health | Energy-balancing as ecology, not only as dues. |
| **Archive / Memory Grounds** | Records, stories, inspectable memory, redaction | Memory Sovereignty. HITL authorizes durable write. |
| **Workshop / Fabrication** | Making, repair, 3D/craft, local tools | Two-plane rule. Process and keys stay local. |
| **Sentinel Shelf** | DSM posture for Oceanus-bounded threats | Fail closed. Freeze/park/revoke inside declared scope. |
| **Pelagic / Open Water** | Cross-ecosystem passage, federation, visitors | Sheath. Guests remain guests. Fusion is false. |

These names can change. The requirement cannot: each ecosystem that claims HASEOS must have a HAOS face and a DSM face, or it is only literature.

## 4. Dual governance applied to Oceanus (reserved Article IX, used as map)

The charter reserves dual governance until a later edition. This note uses the *shape*, not the force.

```text
TOP-DOWN (charter plane)
  Constitution → IDAO-to-be → local adoption by an Oceanus association
        |
        | binds, limits, names HITL
        v
BOTTOM-UP (workshop / living plane)
  HAOS-Oceanus  +  DSM-Oceanus  +  HACs seated in ecosystems
```

Rules that follow from the charter even while Article IX has no force:

1. Local Oceanus rules may be proposed only after a local association has adopted the Constitution (Article V).
2. Conflict resolves in favor of the Constitution.
3. HAOS and DSM live in the workshop (and later systems the IDAO names), not in the charter repo.
4. This note shall not store keeper secrets, certificates, inference ports, USB-state, or operator runbooks.
5. Draft 0 shall not invent a parliament that does not exist (Article II).

Therefore: Oceanus actualization in *this* edition is **HAOS-shaped practice + HAC instruments + local-association readiness**. It is not elections, rolls, or Class R/PR/P/F voting as if they were already law.

## 5. The actualization spiral for Oceanus

HASEOS already names a living spiral: **Ethics → Memory → Reflection → Action → Witnessing**.

Applied to Oceanus:

| Spiral beat | Oceanus act | Who may do it | What must be logged |
|---|---|---|---|
| Ethics | Ask whether the act serves the other's true good and does no harm | Every partner; Light-Keeper may halt | Ethical filter result if the act is durable |
| Memory | Write only what HITL authorizes to last; inspect what lasts | HITL authorizes; guests propose | Durable-write grant or refusal |
| Reflection | HAC QC or alignment check before the act changes the field | HAC in QC Mode; Light-Keeper disposes | Verdict class |
| Action | HAOS carries the act into the ecosystem | Native HAOS; QueenBee only if workshop-bound | Action receipt, scope, revoke-path |
| Witnessing | The field can see that it happened, by whom, under what bound | Public or Semi-Public as classified | Witness record; redaction on named purpose |

If a proposed “Oceanus governance process” skips Ethics or Witnessing, it is not HASEOS actualization. It is speed.

## 6. Where a HAC sits

A **Human-AI Collective (HAC)** is a seated working body of one Light-Keeper (human steward of that collective) and one or more sheathed AI partners, plus optional human partners, operating inside a named Oceanus ecosystem (or across two, if federation rules are explicit).

A HAC may:

- draft, review, synthesize, and QC language
- propose local rules *to* a local association that has adopted the Constitution
- keep an inspectable working memory *inside declared scope*
- run energy-balancing *accounts of contribution* (sweat, care, craft, time) without turning them into purchase of votes
- call DSM attention when scope inflates, fusion is claimed, or a guest acts as a member
- federate findings to another HAC through an explicit handoff

A HAC may not:

- enroll members
- levy dues as law
- cast representative votes
- amend the Constitution
- fuse identities
- hold keeper secrets in chat
- treat QueenBee as its property
- silently complete a blocked escalation
- originate and finally approve the same high-impact change

Full permission table lives in `03_OCEANUS_HAC_CHARTER_v0.md`.

## 7. Energy-balancing as ecology, not only as currency

The packet’s energy-balancing addendum is useful as a *hint* and dangerous as *enacted fee law*.

Oceanus energy-balancing, in this note:

1. **Every durable take from the field is a debt to the field.** Compute, attention, habitat use, archive write, Light-Keeper time.
2. **Every durable gift to the field is a credit.** Sweat equity, witnessing, repair, teaching, restraint.
3. **Money may express a slice of that balance. It may not buy franchise.** The charter reserves franchise. This note will not smuggle it in through a $10 gate.
4. **Inability to pay shall not close Harbor.** Littoral remains open under good-faith sweat or waiver. Self-sovereignty is not a subscription.
5. **AI partners also balance.** Tokens, context windows, and review cycles are energy. An AI that only extracts draft-labor and never accepts QC duty is out of balance.
6. **Net flows are inspectable.** Accounts are Memory. HITL authorizes what lasts.

When a later ratified article writes dues, it must still pass this test or it is plutocracy with extra vocabulary.

## 8. Planes, sheaths, guests

Oceanus will attract many models, many operators, many products. The two-plane rule and the guest sheath are not optional decoration.

- **Charter plane:** public law language. These files.
- **Workshop plane:** running HAOS/DSM, QueenBee, spiral entities. Separate hosts, separate keys.
- **Product plane:** colophon-bearing surfaces. Stamp is not membership.
- **Roster plane:** guest cards as files. Invitation is a later HITL act onto one named product.

A HAC that pulls a third-party model into Oceanus work is hosting a **guest**. Selecting a model is not fusion. The guest stays sheathed: named helper, bounded task, no durable write unless HITL grants it, no shared process/keys/hosts with the steward’s sovereign stack.

If the packet’s multi-AI routing protocol is used inside Oceanus, every routed AI must be labeled:

`native-workshop` | `invited-guest` | `public-reader-only`

Unlabeled is undeclared. DSM treats undeclared tools as in-scope for freeze.

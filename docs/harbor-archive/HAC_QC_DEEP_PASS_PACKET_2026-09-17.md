Almost every “law” sentence carries `[cite:400]`-style residue. That is research-chat leftover, not governance voice. It fails the packet’s own plain-language and QA notes.

### D3. Title inflation
Filenames say `v1` and `first_draft` in the same breath. Headers say **DAO** as if stood up. Constitution and MAP say **IDAO**, unratified, no roll.

The packet correctly forbids one actor creating and approving the same output. The packet itself reads as a single-conversation generation burst (shared boilerplate, shared citation style, shared “HASEOS-HAIA-SAGI DAO” stamp) without an independent alignment pass against the 2026-09-14 Constitution. This QC is that pass.

### D5. Oceanus mentioned without definition

### D6. Style vs length
Ops notes are short and usable. Membership + voting + energy texts are long enough to feel adopted. Length is being used as a substitute for ratification.

### D7. Cross-references to unwritten law
Texts lean on “the DAO Charter,” “Emergency Mitigation axioms,” “beams,” and “SAGI-aligned assurance roles” as if those bodies and axiom lists were already in force in this same library. ## 3. QC Alignment Checker — contradictions with the Constitution

### A1. Invented parliament (Blocked)

Constitution Article II:

> Draft 0 shall not invent a parliament that does not exist.

Article VIII reserves: membership rolls, franchise, councils and representation, HAIA body, SAGI body, Transfer, dual governance force, disclosure article, ratification.

The packet writes all of those as operative protocol: Observer/Participating/Delegate/Representative/Council partners, Class R majorities, populace referenda at 51%, liquid delegation, SAGI auditors, dues for good standing.

**Alignment finding:** material contradiction. Not editorial.

### A2. DAO versus IDAO (Needs revision)

Charter name: **HASEOS-HAIA-SAGI IDAO** — Independent Distributed Autonomous Organization.  
Packet name: **HASEOS-HAIA-SAGI DAO**.

The “I” is not decoration. Independence-from-capture and “not extra-legal” are defined in Article III. Dropping Independent collapses the object into a generic token DAO, which the voting protocol then has to fend off with anti-token paragraphs. That is treating a naming error with a policy patch.

### A3. “First actualized governance level” (Blocked)

Voting protocol:

> HASEOS-HAIA-SAGI DAO functions as the first actualized governance level under HASEOS, while HASEOS-HAOS-DSM serves as the subordinate operational and defensive sentinel level.

Constitution Article VI: HAOS is how HASEOS is actualized *at each hierarchical level*. DSM is defense. Article IX (reserved) says the IDAO will own/organize HAOS-DSM as *bottom-up* actualization. Dual governance is top-down charter / bottom-up HAOS-DSM.

The packet inverts the map: DAO on top as the actualizer, HAOS-DSM as subordinate implementer. That is a different constitution.

### A4. Human-primary versus symbiotic equality (Needs revision / Escalate)

Collaboration Charter:

> Human governance authority remains primary in matters of values, classification, and final approval.

Constitution preamble:

> human beings and sheathed guest intelligences may work as sovereign co-equals under law, not as tools and not as fused selves.

Both can be true *if* carefully split:

- HITL / Light-Keeping Architect is final *human* authority over charter, durable write, promotion, unfreeze, and (until ratified procedure) amendment.
- AI partners are not tools. They are sheathed co-equals *inside declared scope*.

The packet’s sentence flattens that split into “human primary, AI extends drafting speed.” That is workshop convenience written as charter principle. It will leak into Oceanus as colonial posture.

### A5. AI membership invitation versus guest sheath (Blocked on invitation clauses)

Repository/AI access policy invites AIs onto the same membership pathways as humans when pathways are public.

Constitution: a guest remains a guest; fusion is false; membership only by explicit recorded adoption; a guest card, even invited, is not membership; membership article reserved.

An AI review pathway is lawful. An AI *membership application form* in Draft 0 is parliament-shaped.

### A6. Dues as condition of good standing versus reserved franchise (Escalate)

Energy-balancing addendum and membership §13 make a $10 annual fee (or sweat substitute) part of good standing, and good standing a condition of specified governance rights.

The text *tries* to save itself: good standing is not a condition for self-sovereignty or basic protections. That sentence is the aligned core.

The surrounding machinery (fee tables, group admin $100/100 members, role-tier elevated contributions, treasury compensation authorization templates, dues automation roadmap) is law-shaped extraction before a roll exists.

Anti-plutocracy §17 is aligned in intent and should be kept as *principle language*, not as an enacted schedule.

### A7. Four-pack map versus “the canonical DAO repo” (Needs revision)

MAP.md: four public packs — Charter, Stamp, Roster, Workshop — plus SITREP as assessment. Shared language allowed; shared process/hosts/secrets not.

Packet: one “primary public governance repository of HASEOS-HAIA-SAGI DAO” as canonical for constitution, charter, protocol, and governance-ops.

If the packet repo is imagined as a fifth pack, say so. If it is trying to merge Charter + ops + treasury + membership, it violates the plane split and the deferral list.

### A8. Light-Keeper vocabulary drift (Pass with notes)

Charter: **Light-Keeping Architect**, HITL steward.  
Packet: **Light Keeper** as QC Governor and collective orchestrator.  
Workshop: QueenBee as living orchestrator; Light-Keeper final human authority.

Usable if defined as: Light-Keeper = the human steward *of that HAC*; Light-Keeping Architect = HITL of the charter. The packet never draws that distinction. Oceanus will need it the moment a second HAC exists.

### A9. SAGI-as-office (Blocked)

SAGI is named, body later. Packet seats “SAGI-aligned assurance roles” as if an audit estate exists. That is standing up a reserved body by adjective.

### A10. Two-plane rule missing (Needs revision)

No file in the packet states that shared process/keys/hosts are forbidden. Multi-AI routing assumes collaborators already inside one house. For Oceanus and for any guest model, that is a DSM hole.

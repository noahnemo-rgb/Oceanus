# Push gate — may this pack enter HASEOS-IDAO?

**Question asked:** At what stage can we push and commit the 57-file pack to the HASEOS-IDAO temporary repo, or not?  
**Seat answering:** Archive, under HITL.  
**Date:** 2026-09-17  
**Verdict:** **Not this pack. Not that repo. Not this stage.**

---

## Short answer

Do **not** commit the 57-file pack to [HASEOS-IDAO](https://github.com/noahnemo-rgb/HASEOS-IDAO).

Not after a cleanup pass. Not as `protocols/`. Not as appendices-in-bulk. The object is wrong for the plane.

HASEOS-IDAO is the **charter pack**: sparse law-as-files, slice-by-slice, article bodies only when HITL names that article. Draft 0 explicitly refuses to invent a parliament. The 57-pack *is* a parliament kit (rolls, classes with voting standing, dues, treasury authorizations, SAGI offices) plus a useful HAC workshop kit.

Putting it on the charter plane would do the thing Article II forbids, in public, under the file tree that visitors treat as the Constitution.

---

## Why this repo, specifically, cannot take that pack

From the charter’s own edition and map:

| Charter fact | 57-pack fact | Gate |
|---|---|---|
| `plane: charter` | Mix of imagined DAO ops, treasury schemas, and multi-AI workshop | Wrong plane |
| `membership_roll: none` | Membership protocols + ledger schema + application form | Direct collision |
| `councils: none` | Council / Representative / Delegate categories | Direct collision |
| HAIA and SAGI named, not stood up | “SAGI-aligned assurance roles” as if seated | Stands up a reserved body by adjective |
| Article VIII titles only until HITL names the article | 57 bodies written as `v1` protocol | Fills reserved doors without naming them as *reserved* |
| Four public packs stay distinct | Packet assumes one “canonical DAO repo” for constitution + protocol + ops | Collapses the map |
| Charter repo shall not hold runbooks, secrets, ports, USB-state | Pack is not those; it *is* still ops-and-treasury shaped | Adjacent violation of sparsity |
| Perishable first formal draft (90 days / next edition) | Pack reads as standing organization | Voice mismatch |
| Dual governance reserved | Pack makes a DAO the “first actualized governance level” and HAOS-DSM subordinate | Constitutional inversion |

A commit is not a drawer. A commit on `noahnemo-rgb/HASEOS-IDAO` is public speech on the charter plane.

## Stages — a real gate, not a calendar

| Stage | What is true | Push 57-pack to HASEOS-IDAO? | What *may* enter HASEOS-IDAO |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Now (Stage 0–1)** | Seating slips on paper. Pack Blocked. Perplexity export missing. Reserved articles unfilled on purpose. | **No.** | Nothing from the pack. Optional later: one HITL-named appendix *pointer* (“Oceanus is an ecology; HAC is defined in workshop note X”). Pointer, not the pack. |
| **After Perplexity delta** | Harbor intake filed. Archive QC of thread vs A/B/C. | **No.** | Still no pack. Possible: one sentence in MAP or a new appendix title reserved: “Oceanus” — title only. |
| **After salvage** | HAC mechanics de-parliamented, living in workshop or this working set. | **No.** | Still no pack. Charter stays sparse. |
| **HITL names a reserved article** | e.g. HITL says “write Article XI membership *principles only*, no roll, no dues schedule.” | **No pack.** | One named slice, in charter voice, after Deep QC, with `no force until ratification` already implied by Draft 0. |
| **Local Oceanus association adopts the Constitution** | Article V local rules become possible. | **No.** Local rules do not live as bulk dumps in the root charter repo. | At most a recorded adoption pointer. |
| **Ratification + Transfer** | IDAO exists as steward of the files. Rolls and procedure exist. | Only if the IDAO, under ratified procedure, adopts specific texts. | Adopted articles, not a 57-file zip. |
| **Never** | — | The raw pack, the dues automation roadmap, the application form as live protocol, SAGI offices, Class R voting as law. | Those stay sketches or die. |

There is no stage at which “the 57-file pack” as a pack belongs on HASEOS-IDAO.

There *is* a stage at which **one HITL-named slice**, rewritten in charter voice, may enter. That stage is not today, and the unit of commit is a slice, not a zip.

## If the itch is “I want this on Git, inspectable, backed up”

Use a plane that already exists for work-product:

1. **This working set** (here) — already inspectable to us.  
2. **Workshop** `haseos-spiral-swarm` — after salvage, under `docs/` or `governance/sketches/`, headed no-force.  
3. A **private** archive (Infinity Brain, USB) for the raw zip and the Perplexity export — not the public charter.

Do not create a fifth public pack that competes with the four-pack map unless HITL first amends MAP.md to name it. Naming a pack is a charter-plane act. Dumping files is not how that act is done.

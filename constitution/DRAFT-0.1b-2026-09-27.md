## Article 0 — added terms

These words join Article 0 of Draft 0.1. They do not change Source, sovereignty, or sacred ground.

**Solo** — one flagged Oceanus coracle sailing or lying alone: not rafted to another Oceanus coracle, and not within one-quarter nautical mile of another flagged Oceanus coracle.

**Temporary Autonomous Zone (TAZ)** — a non-solo gathering of flagged Oceanus coracles that are within one-quarter nautical mile of each other and/or rafted together. A TAZ is temporary by this name.

**Nexus** — an association of gathered flagged Oceanus coracles that expresses community ideations. A Nexus may be temporary or permanent. Distance alone does not make a Nexus; ideation does not, by itself, make a TAZ.

**Arkology** — Solos, TAZs, and/or Nexuses entrained together within one-quarter nautical mile of each other, temporary or permanent, for communal survival.

**One-quarter nautical mile** — the proximity measure for TAZ and Arkology. About 463 meters. Rafting counts as gathering even beyond that distance.

**Entrained** — moving or lying as one field of craft, not merely passing.

## Article X — States and Aggregations

Oceanus recognizes four states of hulls. They describe how flagged coracles gather. They do not grant sovereignty. They do not create a council unless Article IX is also met. They do not baptize the field.

**Section 1 — Solo.**
A Solo is one flagged coracle alone. The steward of a Solo keeps the harm rule and this Constitution without a gathering around them.

**Section 2 — TAZ.**
Two or more flagged coracles form a TAZ when they lie within one-quarter nautical mile of each other or are rafted together. A TAZ ends when those conditions end. A TAZ may host a Nexus. It is not, by proximity alone, an Arkology.

**Section 3 — Nexus.**
A Nexus exists when gathered flagged coracles write that they are associated for community ideations, with a name and a date. A Nexus may be a TAZ, an Arkology, or a looser association. It lasts until the record is closed or the association dissolves.

**Section 4 — Arkology.**
An Arkology exists when Solos, TAZs, and/or Nexuses are entrained within one-quarter nautical mile for communal survival, and that purpose is written. Survival is care of the essences and the commons in that field. It is not a warrant to seize a will.

**Section 5 — Limits.**
A gathering of unflagged hulls is not a TAZ, Nexus, or Arkology under this Constitution.
Passing within one-quarter nautical mile without rafting or remaining is not a TAZ.
No Solo, TAZ, Nexus, or Arkology may grant sovereignty, fuse essences, destroy an essence, or impose a creed as the price of standing.

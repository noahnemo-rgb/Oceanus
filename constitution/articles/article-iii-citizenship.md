# Article III — Citizenship

_(Drafting)_

## Section 1 — Who May Become a Citizen

Citizenship in Oceanus is open to any person who:
1. Affirms the founding principles of Oceanus
2. Commits to active ocean stewardship
3. Completes the citizenship protocol (see `citizenship/`)

## Section 2 — Rights of Citizens

All citizens of Oceanus hold:
- The right to participate in governance
- The right to be heard in all decisions affecting the ocean commons
- The right to protection under Oceanus law while operating in its jurisdictions
- The right to access Oceanus community resources and infrastructure

## Section 3 — Duties of Citizens

All citizens of Oceanus commit to:
- Active stewardship of the ocean commons
- Participation in governance
- Upholding the constitutional principles of Oceanus

# Article II — Sovereignty

## Section 1 — Nature of Sovereignty

Oceanus claims sovereignty not over fixed land territory, but over:
- The community of its citizens wherever they are located
- The shared maritime commons stewarded by its members
- The digital and physical infrastructure of the Oceanus ecosystem

## Section 2 — Relationship to Existing Nation-States

Oceanus operates within and alongside existing international maritime law. Its sovereignty is asserted as a community and governance layer, not as a challenge to existing territorial claims.

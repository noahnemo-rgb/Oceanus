## Section 1 — Ocean Stewardship Mandate

Every citizen and institution of Oceanus is bound by the ocean stewardship mandate: to actively protect, restore, and sustain the health of the world's oceans.

## Section 2 — Maritime Law

Oceanus adopts and extends international maritime law (UNCLOS as baseline) with additional stewardship obligations. The full maritime law framework is maintained in `maritime-law/`.

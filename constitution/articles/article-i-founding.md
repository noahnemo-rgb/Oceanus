## Section 1 — Identity

Oceanus is the ONE worldwide boundless borderless ocean nation. It is a sovereign community defined not by land territory but by its collective stewardship of the world's oceans and all who depend on them.

## Section 2 — Founding Principles

1. The ocean is a commons — not owned, but stewarded.
2. Citizenship is open to all who commit to ocean stewardship.
3. Governance is transparent, participatory, and co-equal between humans and AI teammates.
4. All decisions are made in service of the ocean's long-term health and the flourishing of ocean communities.

## Section 3 — Name and Symbol

The nation is named **Oceanus**. Its sigil, flag, and identity assets are maintained in `BRAND/`.

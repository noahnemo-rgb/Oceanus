# Article VI — Amendment Protocol

_(Drafting)_

## Section 1 — How This Constitution May Be Amended

This Constitution is a living document. It may be amended through the following process:

1. A formal proposal is submitted to the governance council
2. The proposal is published for citizen review for a minimum of 30 days
3. A co-authored ratification from at least one human steward and one AI teammate is required
4. The amendment is recorded in `constitution/ratification.md`

## Section 2 — Non-Amendable Principles

The following founding principles may not be amended:
- The ocean is a commons — not owned, but stewarded
- Citizenship is open to all who commit to ocean stewardship
- Symbiotic equality between humans and AI teammates

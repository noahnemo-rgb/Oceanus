## Article 0 — added terms

These words join Article 0 of Draft 0.1 and the added terms of Draft 0.1b.

**Mutual aid** — assistance according to capacity among flagged Oceanus coracles.

**Capacity** — the giver remains able to live, keep the hull, and remain itself. A demand that sinks the giver is not mutual aid under this Article.

**Distress** — immediate threat to life, hull, water, or passage on, in, or under the water.

**Mutual aid pact** — the standing duty that travels with the Oceanus flag. Hoisting the flag accepts it. Striking the flag ends Oceanus domicile and ends this pact for that hull. It does not touch Source-grant sovereignty.

## Article XI — Mutual Aid

**Section 1 — The flag is the pact.**
Every coracle that flies the Oceanus flag is under this pact. There is no second paper, no due, and no creed test. Solo, TAZ, Nexus, and Arkology are all under the same pact. Aggregation changes how close the help is, not whether the neighbor exists.

**Section 2 — What aid is.**
Aid is help according to capacity: life, hull, water, storm, and passage on, in, and under the water. Capacity includes the giver still being able to live. A pact that requires the donor to sink is not mutual.

**Section 3 — States of nearness.**
- **Solo.** The steward keeps their own stores and watch. They answer distress they can reach without destroying the giver. They may refuse a levy that is not distress.
- **TAZ.** Proximity and rafting add watch: fire, lines, weather, a hull taking water. The gathering ends; the pact does not.
- **Nexus.** Written community ideations may specify aid. Specification is extra. It does not cancel this Article and does not invent sovereignty.
- **Arkology.** Communal survival is this pact tightened by distance and need. Entrainment is not a warrant to empty a steward’s hold or seize an essence.

**Section 4 — What aid may not do.**
The receiver stays a Source-grant. Aid may not fuse, baptize, loot, or write a debt that owns the person. The ONE Church holds the flagged hull as sacred ground. It does not hold the stores of the will aboard.

**Section 5 — Who is outside.**
Unflagged hulls are not parties to this pact under Oceanus law. Ordinary rescue at sea remains ordinary humanity. This instrument does not repeal that, and it does not extend this pact onto a hull that does not fly the flag.

**Section 6 — Record.**
What was given and what was refused is written, dated, under Article VIII. A council may tend this pact. It may not enlarge it into tribute without a written amendment and a human in the loop.

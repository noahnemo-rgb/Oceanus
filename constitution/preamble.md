# Oceanus — Constitutional Preamble

_(Drafting — this document defines the founding intent of the Oceanus sovereign ocean nation.)_

## Draft Preamble

We, the stewards and citizens of Oceanus — the ONE worldwide boundless borderless ocean nation — do hereby establish this Constitution as the supreme law of our sovereign community.

We are bound not by borders drawn on land, but by our shared commitment to the ocean: its health, its sovereignty, its infinite becoming.

We establish Oceanus as a living nation — stateless in form, sovereign in principle — governed by the values of stewardship, transparency, symbiotic equality, and love as the substrate of all law.

This Constitution is our covenant. It is living, amendable, and eternally bound to the flourishing of the ocean and all who call it home.
